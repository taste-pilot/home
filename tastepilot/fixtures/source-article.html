Field Notes on Slow Publishing

By A. Reader

Slow publishing is not late publishing. It is publishing that respects the time a reader gives it.

Three observations

  1. Readers finish what respects them.
  2. Ornament earns its place or leaves.
  3. A print edition is an interpretation, not an export.
Design is the silent ambassador of the writing.

The lesson survives every format change: measure, rhythm, and restraint.
