The Canon

We don't start from knobs. We start from canons.

A Canon is not a template. It's a reusable editorial grammar — a coherent visual language plus the rules for when and why to use it. Don't ask AI to invent good design. Give it centuries of proven editorial grammar.

  • Modern Editorial

    Confident contemporary long-form. Generous whitespace, expressive drop caps.

  • Swiss Clean

    Grid discipline, restrained palette, typographic clarity.

  • Literary Classic

    Book-inspired measure and rhythm, classical ornament.

  • Technical Journal

    Structured and precise. Built for tables, statistics, and process.

  • Playful Illustrated

    Illustration-forward, lighter rhythm, expressive callouts.

Thousands of styles can make something different.
Taste knows which one to use.
